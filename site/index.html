BrainOS

A voice notebook for the Seeed reTerminal Sticky. Version VERSION.

  1. Plug the Sticky into this computer with a USB-C cable that carries data.
  2. Use Chrome or Edge (Safari and Firefox can't talk to USB).
  3. Click Install, pick the board's port, and wait about 3 minutes.
This browser can't flash over USB: use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Installing replaces the firmware on the board. Seeed's own firmware comes back any time from their Playground. Later updates arrive over Wi-Fi from the board's Settings.

Source and notes: github.com/carcaralivre/brainos. Exported notes are read with the reader page.
